Project Name: Veloura Demo Site

Goal:
Create a premium one-page website for a fictional protein ice cream brand called Veloura.
This is a mock portfolio/demo project meant to showcase high-end branding, polished web design, and business functionality.

Brand Positioning:
Veloura is a modern protein ice cream brand that feels cool, elevated, and desirable.
It is not childish, not overly feminine, and not gym-bro.
It should appeal to both men and women who want dessert that feels worth it and fits modern lifestyle habits.

Tone:
Modern, sharp, tasteful, clean, editorial, slightly aspirational, human.
Avoid robotic wellness language, aggressive fitness language, and generic startup wording.

Design Direction:
- Premium but not overdone
- Soft editorial feel
- Large spacing
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Clean layouts
- Modern luxury mood through visuals rather than over-explaining luxury in text

Color Palette:
- Porcelain #F5F0E8
- Chocolate Brown #4A2010
- Cherry Burgundy #5C1020
- Taupe #A89880
- Butter Cream #F0E8C0

Fonts:
- Playfair Display for headings
- Inter for body

Sections:
1. Sticky header with nav and CTA
2. Hero section
3. Brand story section
4. Product advantage section with macro cards
5. Flavor section with 4 image cards
6. Event cart section
7. Partner strip with text-based fictional brand names
8. Gallery section
9. Contact section with form area and booking CTA
10. Footer

Brand Story Copy:
Headline: Made for cravings. Built for now.
Body:
Veloura started with a simple thought: ice cream should still feel fun, rich, and worth looking forward to — even for people who care about ingredients, protein, and how they eat day to day.

So instead of making something that feels “healthy,” we wanted to make something that feels genuinely good. Better texture. Better flavor. Better balance.

The result is a protein-forward ice cream brand designed for real cravings, elevated events, and modern lifestyles that want both taste and a little intention.
