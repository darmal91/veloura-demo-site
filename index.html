Our story

Made for cravings. Built for now.

We started with a simple thought: ice cream should still feel fun, rich, and worth looking forward to — even when you care about what’s in it.

So we didn’t set out to make something “healthy.” We wanted something that feels genuinely good. Better texture. Better flavor. Better balance.

That’s Veloura: protein-forward ice cream for real cravings, elevated moments, and anyone who wants both taste and a little intention.
